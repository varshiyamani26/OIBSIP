Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

🛡️ Iron Man of India | Freedom Fighter | Nation Builder 🇮🇳

"Manpower without unity is not a strength unless it is harmonized and united properly."

About The Legend

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel was one of the most influential leaders of India's freedom movement. He played a crucial role in integrating over 560 princely states into a united India after independence, earning him the title "Iron Man of India".

Major Achievements

🇮🇳 Freedom Fighter

Played a key role in India's struggle for independence.

🛡️ Iron Man of India

Known for his strong leadership and determination.

🏛️ National Integration

Unified more than 560 princely states into India.

🌍 Inspiration

Inspired generations through patriotism and service.

Life Timeline

1875

Born in Nadiad, Gujarat.

1918

Led the Kheda Satyagraha with Mahatma Gandhi.

1928

Successfully led the Bardoli Satyagraha.

1947

Became India's first Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister.

1947–1950

Integrated princely states into the Indian Union.

1950

Passed away, leaving behind a united India.

Statue of Unity

The Statue of Unity in Gujarat, standing at 182 meters, is the world's tallest statue and was built in honor of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's contributions to India.
